# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We aim to make our community safe, welcoming, and equitable. All members are expected to respect the dignity and rights of others, regardless of identity, background, or experience. Everyone participating in good faith is extended the same privileges.

## Expected Behavior
- Respect the purpose, activities, and culture of our community.
- Interact kindly, honestly, and professionally.
- Honor different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for your actions.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Repair harm when it occurs.
- Support the well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, unwanted attention, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination or stereotyping based on identity traits.
- Sexualized or inappropriate behavior in community spaces.
- Sharing private/confidential information without consent.
- Threats or encouragement of harm.
- Misrepresentation of identity.
- Sharing content without credit or promoting unrelated commercial material irresponsibly.

## Reporting Violations
If you experience or witness a violation, report it to the Community Moderators promptly. Reports are taken seriously, investigated confidentially, and resolved with safety and fairness as priorities.

## Enforcement and Repair
Moderators may use the following measures depending on severity:

1. **Warning:** Private notice, apology or clarification of expectations.
2. **Limited Activities:** Temporary restrictions on communication or participation.
3. **Suspension:** Temporary removal from community spaces with conditions to return.
4. **Permanent Ban:** Complete removal from the community for severe or repeated violations.

The enforcement ladder is flexible and may skip steps based on impact.

## Scope
Applies to all community spaces and when representing the community externally (social media, official email, events).
